# Problem

Building Fabrication 2 facade runs are primarily authored and consumed as straight segments, with limited special-case curvature. This makes a narrower front face with smoothly curving adjacent faces difficult to express generically. Treating a curved face as if it were flat at its midpoint would also misplace walls, openings, balconies, trim, roof edges, and attachments. Terra & Mar first needs the lower-risk stage-1 solution from AI 537 that joins adjacent balconies; general custom face paths are a later, separate refactor.

# Request

After AI 537 is complete, add an opt-in, path-aware facade system that supports custom spline-shaped faces through one shared resolved-path contract, while preserving existing straight and circular-arc buildings and making unsupported builder behavior explicit and validated.

Tasks:
- Define canonical schema, model, normalization, and specification semantics for straight, circular-arc, and custom spline facade paths. Preserve stable facade/run ids, endpoint identity, reversal, linking, mirroring, repeat behavior, and deterministic serialization across round trips.
- Expose one resolved facade-path API to consumers, including position, unit tangent, outward normal, and arc-length mapping at normalized `u`, plus total length and deterministic endpoint behavior. Define how reversal changes `u`, tangent, normal, linked endpoints, and ids without changing the represented geometry.
- Define robust offset-curve behavior for wall thickness, balcony depth, fascia and belt offsets, parapets, and other parallel geometry. Specify validation and deterministic fallback/error behavior for self-intersections, cusps, excessive offsets, degenerate spans, discontinuous joins, and invalid control points.
- Add adaptive tessellation and LOD rules with bounded geometric error, stable seams, predictable normals, and no visible cracks or topology changes at linked face endpoints. Include performance budgets and diagnostics so custom curves do not silently create unbounded vertex counts or frame-time regressions.
- Add Building Fabrication 2 editor authoring for custom face paths, including creation/conversion controls, selectable control points and handles, tangent/continuity controls, endpoint snapping/linking, reversal, numeric editing, preview, validation feedback, undo/redo, copy/paste, and import/export round trips.
- Unify the silhouette popup and main face editor's outward-curve sign calculation behind one shared, tested helper. Derive outward bulge from the resolved winding and face-normal side instead of hardcoding an area-sign assumption; Terra's positive-area silhouette must use a positive bulge to curve outward. Add regressions for positive and negative winding and for reversed runs so both editors produce the same outward curve before and after reversal.
- Specify path-aware UVs, material continuity, cuts, doors, windows, repeated openings, and opening-width/spacing semantics in arc-length space. Openings and cuts must follow local position/tangent/normal throughout their extent rather than being placed on a flat midpoint approximation.
- Introduce a builder capability contract. Every facade-consuming builder must declare either path-aware support for each accepted path kind or a validated unsupported state that blocks or clearly reports incompatible authoring. Do not allow silent flat, chord-based, or midpoint geometry for a curved path.
- Migrate and validate consumers in scoped phases: wall surfaces and corner joins; doors/windows/openings and cuts; belts, cornices, reveals, and facade trim; balcony slabs, fascia, guards, and linked balcony endpoints; roof edges, parapets, and gutters; then facade attachments and remaining registered builders. Keep each phase testable and backward compatible before proceeding.
- Keep existing straight and circular-arc behavior visually and numerically compatible unless an explicitly documented defect is corrected. Provide migration handling for existing configs and reject ambiguous legacy data with actionable diagnostics.
- Add schema, model, UI, generator, serialization, validation, and builder-contract tests. Cover reversed paths, mirrored/repeated layers, linked endpoints, arc-length placement, offsets, openings across tessellation boundaries, LOD changes, invalid splines, unsupported builders, deterministic export/import, and legacy straight/arc configurations.
- Add focused visual and performance acceptance scenes for straight, circular-arc, and custom spline faces at representative and stress workloads. Capture comparisons, seam close-ups, opening alignment, balcony/trim continuity, LOD transitions, and failure diagnostics under `tests/artifacts/screens/ai539-custom-facade-paths/`; keep all generated images, traces, manifests, logs, and reports gitignored.
- Only after the stage-1 adjacent-balcony joining work from AI 537 is complete and verified, add an optional Terra & Mar showcase variant with a narrower front face and smoothly curved adjacent faces. Use it to prove that walls, openings, balcony joins, rails, trim, and roof edges all follow the same resolved path; do not make the curved variant a prerequisite for the stage-1 Terra fix.
- Update the relevant canonical building, facade, window/balcony, editor, schema, and testing specifications with the final supported contract, phased compatibility matrix, limitations, and acceptance evidence.
