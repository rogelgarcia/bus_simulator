What changes?

Detailed canopy v10: LOD3/4 retain individual LOD2 blades, distributed in small ground cells, with prepared width and color compensation. This removes the large tuft-card pattern while retaining dark roots and leaf detail. It costs more geometry than cards but fewer triangles than LOD2, with no card atlas or billboard projection for these levels. LOD5 keeps the two 1K texture tiles, restores anisotropic filtering and preserves leaf/cavity contrast in its mipmaps for a more detailed far surface. Its palette is calibrated against LOD4. Prepared mip colors match the far blade contrast, and perimeter vertex colors darken the wall toward its roots. Filtering uses up to 16× anisotropy. The final blend shares a small view-ray calculation to avoid coverage gaps, with no extra texture reads, geometry or draw calls. Earlier versions remain selectable in the comparison.

Switches: 0.6 / 0.8 / 20 / 30 / 40 m. LOD3 covers 20–30 m, LOD4 30–40 m, LOD5 beyond 40 m. The latest profile blends over 14.24–20, 27–30 and 37–40 m, finishing at those limits. Selection stays at 50 ms and 0.2 m. Root shading approximates self-occlusion; one cached lookup retains external shadows. Direct rendering uses MSAA without bloom. The shadow cache is static and must be rebuilt if objects or the sun move.
