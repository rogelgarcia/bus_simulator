Interior canopy plates + exterior leaf cards

Each tree has five interior centers with three crossing planes each. These plates supply the crown mass. Smaller exterior planes, each carrying six separate leaves, shape the silhouette. Every foliage plane is flat and double-sided. Wood is reduced to half the preceding LOD0 budget and rebaked from the detailed reference.

Resource costs across all 15 models

Large plates save geometry but require larger textures. Memory assumes shared canopy textures and ASTC 4×4 or BC7. Loading every GLB independently uses more memory. Material draws are mesh primitives before any renderer batching; frame time and FPS have not been measured in the game.

Review conditions and limits

Shaded: 1920 × 1080, Cycles, 64 samples, denoising, RTX 3060 / OPTIX. Identical cameras, HDRI, sun, ground and exposure. Final UASTC-decoded textures. Reference and previous LOD0 captures are authenticated copies with matching camera metadata. Wireframes use the actual exported triangles, 32 samples, and a separate dark diagram background.

Interior projections reduce depth and may reveal plane intersections at close range. Both faces share tissue color; front/back normals reverse correctly and the 0.17 diffuse-transmission factor is retained. Fine twig geometry, independent leaf parallax and wind are absent. Texture memory rises to retain large-plate detail. Gameplay integration, frame time and FPS are not measured.
