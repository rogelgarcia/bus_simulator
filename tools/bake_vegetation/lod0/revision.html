Review conditions and limits

1920 × 1080 matched Cycles renders, 64 samples, denoising, OPTIX on RTX 3060. Identical cameras, HDRI, sun, ground and exposure. The new renders use the final UASTC-decoded textures. The authenticated reference/current images reuse the preceding review captures with matching camera and sample metadata.

Pruning checks alpha silhouettes from 24 directions at 512 × 512 and allows at most 0.25% coverage loss in each direction. This is a geometric guard, not a runtime performance result. Close-up parallax remains below the solid-leaf reference. Wind, lower LODs and gameplay integration remain separate work; frame time and FPS are not measured.
