# Photographic surface review revision (AI586)

All five species and their three mature forms require real photo-derived PBR
sources, replacing the earlier procedural bark relief and leaf vein fields.
The `vegetation/surfaces` bake leaf produces a separate editable candidate scene
and reusable PBR map packs. This detail-first review revision does not replace
licensed game trees or certify the existing optimized/exported library.

The clean fused woody skeleton is rebuilt through the existing voxel union,
root cap, fragment and topology checks. No old surface displacement or ring
scar survives. Authenticated scan heights produce bounded geometric relief;
registered triplanar color/roughness/height supply the fine bark response without
branch-cylinder UV seams. Source normal maps are preserved in each map pack.
Scan offsets and modest scale variation are independently seeded per specimen.
Four registered photographic crops extend the bark repeat vertically; the same
crop transitions apply to every channel. Leaf map padding is derived from actual
tissue pixels, avoiding black borders when the solid outline is reshaped.

Closed modeled leaf shells sample scan silhouettes and geometry height, with
irregular curl and botanical outline adaptations. Leaf material opacity remains
one. Photographed vein networks and tissue variation replace periodic formulas.
Each species supplies color, OpenGL normal, roughness, height and a source mask
for future plate authoring. The mask is used for tracing, not current rendering.
Front/back appearances are artistic adaptations; no claim of calibrated leaf
transmission or exact species photometric measurement is made.

London plane uses a Platanus bark scan. Linden uses generic fissured bark;
northern red oak uses Jolcham oak bark; elm uses a willow bark proxy; arrowwood
uses the existing generic photographed bark. Leaf tissue sources are anatomical
proxies and are recorded individually in the tool recipes. Reference photos
remain in downloads with their individual licenses; none with restricted reuse
are copied into runtime maps.

Acceptance: 15 specimens, five isolated plots, retained original canopy anchors,
closed finite leaf templates, manifold wood, authenticated source maps, no
procedural noise material nodes, 26 Cycles cameras (overview and five views per
species: lineup, hero, trunk, roots, front/back leaf study). Final review needs
visual inspection of every species and its surface close-ups. The shared bake
framework enforces stable inputs and rejects publication from this review leaf.
